Document the doubts in your safety case.

Defeater Cards are a structured record for defeaters in safety assurance cases: why a doubt matters, where it came from, when it applies, and how it was resolved. They attach to any notation without changing it.

In a typical safety case

"Unless sensor disagreement under partial occlusion or glare occurs, leading to false or missed detections."

One sentence. No source, rationale, scope or resolution.

With a Defeater Card

Six questions, one record

Each card answers the 5W1H questions about a defeater, plus a history of how it changed. Fields map to sixteen documentation criteria (R1–R16) derived from the literature.
